## Introduction

ReadyIntelligence usage is measured in **credits**. Your plan gives you an allowance for the
current **period** (monthly or annually). The **Credit usage** screen in the admin shows how
much of that allowance you have used, how much remains, and a breakdown by activity.

You can also ask the [admin assistant](./admin-assistant.md) about this period's usage — for
example "How much of our credit allowance have we used this month?"

## What the usage screen shows

| Area | What it tells you |
|------|-------------------|
| **Current period** | The billing window these figures apply to, including start and end dates. |
| **Limits** | Your allowance for the period and the total cap. |
| **Usage this period** | Credits consumed so far, and how many remain. |
| **Usage by activity type** | How credits were spent, grouped by activity. |

Typical activity types include:

- **Assistant chat** — conversations in website assistants and the admin assistant.
- **Document indexing** — reading and indexing content into the [knowledge base](./knowledge-base.md).

Indexing a large source, or a busy period of chat, will show up here as a rise against those
activities.

## When you reach the cap

If usage hits the period cap:

- New **assistant chat** may be refused until credits are available again.
- **Document indexing** pauses. Documents already being processed may still finish and consume
  credits; new documents are not sent for processing until there is allowance left.

The admin shows a notice when indexing is paused for this reason. Contact your
ReadyIntelligence contact if you need the allowance reviewed.
